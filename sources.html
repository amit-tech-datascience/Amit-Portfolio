What the numbers mean

PCB2 scores describe a custom development split with 50 anomalous and 50 normal images, using independently calibrated configurations. An independent final evaluation remains pending. The landslide scores are collaborative course-project results on a subset and local split, not official challenge scores. Aggregate evaluations were not rerun for this website. One false-alarm overlay was regenerated from the frozen inspection model and checked against its saved score, crop and model hash.

Research and collaboration

Landslide work was conducted with Reiko Lettmoden. The thesis adapts existing PINN frameworks. Neural style transfer, text generation and introductory ML repositories are labeled coursework. The DLR contribution is a coauthored conference speech; no peer-reviewed journal claim is made.

Visuals and privacy

Conceptual diagrams are labeled. The inspection and landslide charts render documented results. The inspection gallery contains three real VisA inputs and saved model overlays, with creator attribution and CC BY 4.0 licensing. Cropping, resizing and overlay modifications are disclosed; scores are not probabilities. The public two-page résumé was prepared from Amit’s supplied résumé and current professional profile. Personal records, certificates, signatures, home addresses, phone numbers, private code and workplace materials are excluded.
