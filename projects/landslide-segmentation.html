Collaborative academic project · 2023

Mapping landslides from multispectral imagery

Comparing convolutional and transformer models for a small, highly imbalanced class in satellite imagery.

StatusCompleted academic collaboration

Tools & methodsSemantic segmentation, UNet, Swin, SegFormer

EvidenceRead the team report ↗

The problem

Landslide pixels form a small minority in satellite images. A segmentation model must distinguish them from a large background class.

Conceptual illustration, not an experimental output.

My contribution

In a TU Braunschweig team project with Reiko Lettmoden, we compared UNet, a Swin-based UNet and SegFormer, studying input bands, augmentation and loss functions.

The approach

The Landslide4Sense inputs combine multispectral and terrain information. We used a subset of the original training data with a local split, and compared cross-entropy and Lovász-based training.

Results & learning

The report records F1 0.756 for augmented UNet with Lovász loss, versus 0.721 for baseline UNet: a 3.5 percentage-point difference in our project experiments.

What this establishes

Team results, not an individual benchmark claim. Experiments used 50% of the original training data and a custom split; scores are not official challenge test results.
