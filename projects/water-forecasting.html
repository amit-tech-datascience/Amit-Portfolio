Earlier applied ML · 2022

Learning from water and weather

An early exploration of hydrological forecasting through aquifer, lake and river notebooks.

StatusEarlier learning project

Tools & methodsPandas, XGBoost, Time series

EvidenceExplore the water analytics notebooks ↗

The problem

Hydrological time series contain missing values and different temporal patterns. Forecasting requires careful preparation and evaluation over time.

Conceptual illustration, not an experimental output.

My contribution

I explored the Acea Smart Water Analytics data in notebooks covering aquifer, lake and river observations.

The approach

The aquifer notebook uses cleaning, temporal resampling, a chronological split and XGBoost regression to estimate groundwater depth from weather and hydrological inputs.

Results & learning

This work established practical foundations in data preparation, time-series analysis and tabular predictive modeling.
