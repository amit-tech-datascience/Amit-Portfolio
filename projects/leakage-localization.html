Academic project · 2023

Using symmetry to locate leakage

Comparing standard networks, augmentation and symmetry-aware models when training data is limited.

StatusCompleted academic experiment

Tools & methodsTensorFlow, Equivariance, Regression

EvidenceExplore the experiment notebooks ↗

The problem

Predicting a leakage location should respond consistently when the input and its coordinate system are rotated or reflected.

Conceptual illustration, not an experimental output.

My contribution

I explored neural leakage-localization models in an academic project, comparing standard and equivariant networks.

The approach

The repository contains experiments with 100 and 1,000 training examples, with and without rotation/reflection augmentation, along with Keras implementations and model-comparison notebooks.

Results & learning

The experiments compare architectural symmetry with learned invariance from augmented examples, using coordinate regression as the task.
