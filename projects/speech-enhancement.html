Reconstructing clearer speech

The problem

Speech enhancement must address noise while preserving the qualities that make speech natural and intelligible.

Conceptual illustration, not an experimental output.

My contribution

I worked on an academic project combining contrastive predictive coding representations and HiFi-GAN in PyTorch.

The approach

Experiments used speech data including LJ Speech and Libri-Light, with an EffCRN baseline. Evaluation considered complementary quality measures: NISQA, PESQ and DNSMOS.

Results & learning

The project developed my experience in audio modeling, representation learning and systematic evaluation across speech-quality metrics.
